For the 11 m citizens band, freeband and shortwave listening — not the upstream SDRoxide.

The radio you point at the 11 m band, or at a shortwave station that hasn't said anything in six hours.

Everything upstream SDRoxide does, plus the parts a listener and a CB operator actually want: WSJT-CB callsigns and channel plans, the broadcast schedule, the reception log, and a screen that works on a phone.

Which build am I?

Both builds install a program called sdroxide. That is the whole reason this page exists.

Run this and read the line

sdroxide --version

SDR Oxide Brown … 2.0.12_brown  ← Brown, this site
SDR Oxide … 1.6.10  ← upstream, a different program

The title bar and Settings → General both show it too. If yours does not say _brown, you are running upstream and this page is not about your build.

What's different

Brown is upstream plus this. Nothing upstream has been taken away.

11 m as a two-way band

WSJT-CB interop and channel plans, FT8 working 19 callbook calls, the wide CB grammar the community has outgrown, and the transmit etiquette that goes with it.

The listener's side

Broadcast schedule from EiBi, Aoki or HFCC · reception log with QSL tracking · time-shift replay · scheduled recordings · ECSS · signal identification.

Digital broadcast

DAB/DAB+ decoded and played, with station name, programme type and MOT slideshow.

Working the radio

Contest logger · ACARS · KNOWN window (which hashes this station can resolve) · a real iambic CW keyer that keys your rig.

The screen

Detachable windows you can put on a second monitor · saved workspaces · a phone and tablet layout · a browser client that signs in once.

Cheap receivers

The ATS Mini and other sound-card-only boards are supported as listening receivers, alongside the RTL-SDR, RSP1, HackRF and the rest.
